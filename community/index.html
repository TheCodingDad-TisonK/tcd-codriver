Community

Drivers who switch on the community board in TCD Co-Driver choose to share a few totals here: a display name, level, title, badges, average score, deliveries and distance. Everything else stays on their own computer.

How to join

In the plugin, open Settings, switch on the community board and choose a display name. Your entry appears after the next delivery and updates as you drive. Switch it off at any time and your entry is removed straight away. Nobody is on the board unless they turn it on themselves.
